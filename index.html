GRAMMAR LAB · VERB BE

Meet am · is · are ✨

Choose the correct verb depending on the subject.

SUBJECT + am / is / are + INFORMATION
🙋

I + am

I am Peruvian.

👩 👨 🐈

🎯 Quick oral check: You → are · He → is · We → are · I → am.
GRAMMAR LAB · CONTRACTIONS

Shorter, more natural speech 💬

A contraction joins the subject and the verb in everyday speaking.

🙋

I am → I'm

I'm Milagritos.

🫵

You are → You're

You're my teacher.

👩

She is → She's

She's Peruvian.

👨

He is → He's

He's a doctor.

🐈

It is → It's

It's a cat.

👫

We are → We're

We're friends.

👥

They are → They're

They're students.

PRONUNCIATION · SOUND FOCUS

Two different sounds: /ɪ/ vs /iː/ 🎧

Listen to the vowel sound. The difference is not only the written letter.

👂

/ɪ/ · short vowel

is /ɪz/ · it /ɪt/ · sit /sɪt/

Short and relaxed: no long stretch.

🗣️

/iː/ · long vowel

he /hiː/ · she /ʃiː/ · see /siː/

Longer, tenser vowel. Smile slightly.

He is here.

he /iː/ vs is /ɪ/

She is a teacher.

she /iː/ vs is /ɪ/

It is big.

it /ɪ/ vs is /ɪ/

🎯 Mini challenge: Say he – is – he – is, then He is. Listen and compare your vowels.
